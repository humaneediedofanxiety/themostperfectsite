He has something to say to me.

Mahmudul Hasan - June 1st, 2025 - quick read.

I won't know how to describe today, I was overwhelmed by different feelings at different times but I must say now that I feel quite relaxed as of the time of writing, pardon my mistakes.

I would want to write a small piece of text in second-person directed to me as to easy my problems and find relief. It has been a tough day.

"Dear young man, do not live life in regret as if you have reached the end of your life without achieveing anything as it's not the end but the very beginning, only a little time has passed since you have come to your senses, since you have been capable of thought so I please and I beg, do not beat yourself up for it, there is time and you must not waste this time in regret, live your life I repeat. You are not to think too lowly for yourself for that is a crime nor must you think too highly of yourself for that you are human too and you must die. You are not to live your life in isolation, we are social creatures and so are you. Other people have vast amount of experiences, often more than you and if all you do is keep to yourself, you are missing out on everything that they have to offer, human-connection is very much important but also remember there is also an extreme to this, when you are utterly dependant on other people, you have no individual thought, you are not you but only a part of society. And on the opposite, you have got the isolated man, he thinks, he has all the time to think but he lacks experiences and he lacks connection, all he ever does is think and dream and he is afraid that other people are repulsed by him. I would stronly suggest you be somewhere in the sweet spot, of course you will always be more inclined to one of the extremes because 'balance is impossible' and I would also suggest that you incline a bit more to the 'isolated man' rather than lose all your individuality for I find the 'isolated man' more prideful than the latter. Of course everything is I say is simply my perception of things and an opinion. So think for yourselves too, young soul. Do not ever think you are not worthy of joy, of happiness. You are just as worthy as anybody else, so you must not feel guilty as you have not wronged anyone to achieve it. For you must not escape from discomfort, pain, suffering. First of all, you will never escape and secondly, there is virtue in it and what good is happiness if not for grief. And dear young man, I must bid farewell now and I will reappear in times of your biggest distress."

It was a tough day and I am grateful for it.
