How kind of a man you were and I never knew your name.

Mahmudul Hasan - June 3rd - quick read.

How kind of a man you are and I haven't even known your name. I have read the book "White Nights" and I might watch the drama too, is this the true definition of love or was it simply an example of a failed romance, was it a warning to us all? I simply do not know and I must find out.

I have had a very average day, I didn't feel like doing anything today and truth be told I did not much today, only a few simple things and what I tell you is that I must reduce the amount of things, maybe cut back on movies a bit and only read books for a while and maybe hyperfocus on web-engineering and learn everything there is and build various things. Maybe, live life.

I am really sleepy and I must sleep today but dare me I haven't forgotten about it, I have something to say to a friend of mine, how has she forgotten me and how angry am I at her for forgetting me so simply. I must write to her but I will write tomorrow.
